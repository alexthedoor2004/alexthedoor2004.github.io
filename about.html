About

The short version.

I grew up in Ottawa, switching between English and French depending on who was in the room.

I went to Waterloo for science, with biotech in the mix, and ended up adding accounting and finance on top. The biotech-to-finance jump made more sense than it sounds: the first real deal I worked on was helping a biotech startup raise money, and I liked being the person who could talk to both sides.

Co-op took me through tax at EY, forensic work at Deloitte, catching money laundering at Finance Canada, and audit at BNP Paribas in Montreal. Different places, same question underneath: where is the money going, and does the story match the numbers?

Then I wanted to be closer to where things get built. I joined Waterloo's student venture fund and spent the summer of 2026 in San Francisco doing growth at Finta, a YC company, where nobody hands you a process and you just figure it out.

When I'm not working I'm usually travelling, or planning the next trip.
