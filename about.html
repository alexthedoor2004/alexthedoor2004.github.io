about

I grew up in Ottawa, switching between English and French depending on who was in the room.

I went to Waterloo for science and ended up adding accounting and finance on top.

Co-op took me through corporate tax at EY, anti-money laundering at Finance Canada, and forensic work at Deloitte. Different places, same question underneath: where is the money going, and does the story match the numbers?

Then I wanted to be closer to where things get built. I joined Waterloo's student venture fund and spent the summer of 2026 in San Francisco doing growth at Finta, a YC company, where nobody hands you a process and you just figure it out.

When I'm not working I'm usually travelling, or planning the next trip.
